TwinkBot

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains how TwinkBot processes Discord data to provide community participation streaks, role alerts, and automatic discussion threads.

TwinkBot is operated by @spiderog on Discord. This policy covers those features and this policy website.

1. Information we process

  • Discord identifiers and member information: server, user, role, and channel IDs; usernames, display names, server nicknames, and role membership used to operate configured features.
  • Streak records: current and best streak counts, the last qualifying activity date, base and last-applied nicknames, nickname conflict and retry information, and member preferences.
  • Server configuration: streak settings and eligibility filters, reward roles, trusted nickname editors, alert permissions and cooldown timestamps, and automatic thread rules.
  • Message data: message text and metadata, attachment and embed information, links, emojis, and mentions used to evaluate automatic thread rules. Streaks use message activity to determine whether a member participated on a qualifying day.
  • Operational information: command and interaction details, relevant identifiers, timestamps, and errors used to operate and troubleshoot the bot. When interaction audit logging is enabled, command names and parameters and interaction details may be sent to a configured Discord logging channel.

Nickname conflict handling may use Discord audit-log events to identify who changed a nickname. The bot does not need your Discord password or account token.

2. How information is used

We use this information to calculate streaks, display leaderboards, maintain nickname markers, assign configured streak reward roles, send announcements, check alert permissions and cooldowns, and create threads that match server rules. Operational information helps diagnose failures and maintain these features.

We do not sell Discord data, use it for targeted advertising, or use message content to train machine learning or AI models.

3. Storage and message content

Streak records, preferences, and server configuration are stored outside Discord in the bot's PostgreSQL database. The bot also temporarily processes Discord data in memory while it runs.

The streak, alert, and automatic thread features do not maintain a database archive of ordinary message text or attachment files. Automatic thread matching processes message data as messages arrive. A configured thread name may include text from the original message; that name is then stored and displayed within Discord.

Operational logs may contain identifiers and error details. Discord messages, thread names, announcements, and audit messages remain subject to Discord's storage and the server's permissions.

4. Who can see information

Streak markers, leaderboards, reward roles, announcements, alerts, and thread names can be visible to members according to the server's configuration and Discord channel permissions. Configured audit logs are visible to people who can access their logging channel.

The bot operator and infrastructure providers may process data as needed to host, maintain, or troubleshoot the service. Information may also be disclosed when required by law. Discord independently processes information under its Privacy Policy.

5. Your choices

Use /streak to manage available preferences for nickname markers, announcements, and announcement pings. These preferences affect how your streak is displayed; they do not stop streak tracking or reward-role processing.

There is no individual switch that opts you out of all message processing. Server managers can configure streak eligibility filters, disable features, restrict the bot's channel access, or remove the bot. Automatic thread rules apply to the locations configured by server managers.

For access, correction, deletion, or help stopping future processing, contact @spiderog on Discord. Include your Discord user ID and the relevant server, and explain your request. We may need to verify that the request concerns your account. Do not send passwords or tokens.

6. Retention and deletion

Persistent records support ongoing streak history, preferences, and server settings. There is no fixed automatic expiration period for these records. Leaving a server, deleting a Discord message, or disabling a display preference does not automatically delete database records.

Deletion requests are handled by the operator. Deleting a stored record does not by itself prevent a new record from being created if you continue participating while tracking remains enabled; contact us to coordinate any needed exclusion with server managers. Deleting bot database records also does not automatically delete existing messages or thread names in Discord.

7. This website

This policy page does not include application analytics, advertising trackers, forms, or scripts that collect Discord data. The website hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser information, to deliver and protect the page.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when features or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

Privacy questions or requests?

Contact @spiderog on Discord. If you cannot reach the operator directly, ask your community's server staff to help you contact them about TwinkBot.
